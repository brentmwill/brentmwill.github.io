Bag End Fitz

A private household tool. Not a public service.

Bag End is a home dashboard that one household runs on its own server. Fitz is the part of it that helps one member of that household keep track of his own activity and sleep.

Fitz has exactly one user: the person who set it up. It is not offered to anyone else, there is nothing to sign up for, and it is not available for download.

With that person's permission, Fitz reads his activity and sleep data from the Google Health API. It only reads; it never writes to or changes anything in his Google account.
